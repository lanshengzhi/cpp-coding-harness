## Established behavior and boundaries

Rows retain their ledger ticket and separate plan mapping, receipt provenance, and TUI batch information. The null/draft lists include all rows; a separate historical list includes supported missing/partial rows. The roll-up counts ledger membership, implementation and verification independently. Open tickets without rows cover fetched hierarchy and explicitly referenced issues; rows are associated by their current ledger ticket, plan mappings, delivery receipts and recorded TUI batch mappings. Blocker edges are reported, not interpreted as successor edges. Consequently closed-ticket rows are identified as having **unknown** successor status, not as having no successor. The wiki lint checks relative Markdown link paths and queries tracker references not already in the fetched graph in batches. It does not resolve Markdown anchors or validate external URLs.

The historical table reproduces the published proposal table as text; it does not classify current rows or claim its counts were validated. The [proposal](../../wiki/pi-alignment/reconciliation-1183-proposal-2026-11-04.md) §2 says all 341 rows are missing/partial but includes three implemented codemode rows. Its execution note also records a later owner ruling of 16 rows for the reservation/admission group originally counted as 11. A direct count of the committed ledgers gives 191 null and 149 draft rows at `6ca7b493c`, and 192 null and 149 draft at `a4df1a1153b044f3e406cf543d36f3ad1180c864`, restricted to supported missing/partial rows. The latter is a plausible reconstruction of the withdrawal inputs, but the original live issue graph and a complete original row-to-group assignment have not been recovered. The live issue graph today is not evidence of its 2026-11-04 state. An owner ruling or a recovered dated graph and row classification is needed to establish the historical acceptance criterion, and explicit successor evidence is needed to determine which closed rows lack an open successor.
