## Established behavior and boundaries

Rows retain their ledger ticket and separate plan mapping, receipt provenance, and TUI batch information. The null/draft lists include all rows; a separate historical list includes supported missing/partial rows. The roll-up counts ledger membership, implementation and verification independently. Open tickets without rows cover fetched hierarchy and explicitly referenced issues; rows are associated by their current ledger ticket, plan mappings, delivery receipts and recorded TUI batch mappings. Closed-ticket rows have three categories: known open documented successor, checked with no documented open successor, and insufficient information. Only direct relationships in [the successor mapping](../../../scripts/evidence/1058-successors.json) count; parentage and blockers never imply succession. Each entry names a source and states whether its documented direct successor list is complete. An absent or incomplete entry cannot establish that no documented open successor exists; a complete entry reports only the result of that documented check, not proof that no successor exists anywhere. Mapped successors are fetched even outside the hierarchy; GitHub errors fail the whole report. The mapping currently records the owner decisions for #1176→#1205 and #1177→#1206 and makes no exhaustive claims. The wiki lint checks relative Markdown link paths and queries tracker references not already in the fetched graph in batches. It does not resolve Markdown anchors or validate external URLs.

The historical comparison reads the ledger at candidate commit `a4df1a1153b044f3e406cf543d36f3ad1180c864` using `git show`, independently of the current ledger and live graph. The default requires that Git object to exist in the checkout's repository; shallow clones and source archives must obtain the object or use `--historical-ledger PATH` to provide an alternative JSON ledger, which is explicitly reported as unverified caller input. The [proposal](../../wiki/pi-alignment/reconciliation-1183-proposal-2026-11-04.md) used an uncommitted tree and then-live graph: the candidate reproduces its 341-row population, but does not prove historical identity. All 17 groups are reported individually with published counts, defensible candidate row IDs and group-specific boundaries; complete selections include a count delta. Partial candidates may overlap and are not a partition. Groups 3, 6–8, 10 and 17 match their published counts in this candidate; count agreement alone does not prove historical membership. Group 16 remains partial because its terminal-row assignments are unresolved. Group 9 explicitly identifies the three implemented #1193 rows outside the stated missing/partial population; group 13 identifies the withdrawn MCP row outside it. The execution note records a later owner ruling of 16 reservation/admission rows versus the original 11. The original live graph and full original assignment remain unavailable. The user accepted this explicit historical boundary instead of the original strict historical match; no historical issue-state equivalence is claimed.
