## Findings, blockers, environment improvements

- **Findings.** The ledger lag is structural, not accidental: slices deliberately declined
  promotion and #1180 owns receipts, but the ticket-reference layer (`#1058-draft`/null) was never
  reconciled, so the lag now hides real progress (defineDoc-class rows) and real gaps (21 unobserved
  durable rows) alike. The 192/149 rows have no live owner reference at all, which fails #1183's
  acceptance criterion 1 as written today. Closed slices without reports (#1081) are the weakest
  evidence class and should not back E2 edits without review.
- **Blockers.** E0 (owner decision on the uncommitted tree); the #1104/#1202 dispositions; the
  symlink-divergence ruling; filing N1–N6 (or the owner's alternative routing) before E1/E2 can
  land; #1167 (test reliability) still gates #1126.
- **Environment improvements** (extending the [residual-gap audit](residual-gap-audit-2026-11-04.md)):
  (1) the §4 script, checked in under `scripts/`; (2) a `consolidate_audits.py` warning (not error)
  for null/`#1058-draft`/closed-ticket references; (3) the automated #1202 boundary check in the
  evidence suite; (4) a delivery-report requirement in the close-out path so #1081-class slices
  cannot close with only a commit; (5) a wiki link/tracker lint; (6) a warm build and a usable
  `PI_CHECKOUT` so receipt re-runs take minutes, not a day.
